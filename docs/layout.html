Dictate

The wide clear bar sends Control+Shift+D once per tap. Configure your dictation tool to toggle recording globally and insert into the focused app. The helper adds no paste. Superwhisper is the maintainer's choice, not a required dependency.

Three useful distinctions

Copy response presses the latest assistant response's Copy control. Joystick ⌘C copies your selection.

Y / X act on a visible permission request. Y is Allow once, never Always allow. Missing or ambiguous controls produce no approval.

Layer switching changes apps/views. Other helper actions follow the app currently in front, even if it differs from the selected layer.
